# THE ECOSYSTEM: FEATURES & MECHANICS

## I. Hybrid Setoran Engine & The "Mushaf Architecture"
*Standard: Kurikulum Madinah & Makkah (Talaqqi & Sanad)*
- **Smart Stopping (Ruku' & Maqra'):** When users do "Koreksi Mandiri" or submit a Setoran, the AI prevents them from stopping mid-sentence. The UI guides them to stop exactly at a **Ruku'** (Thematic end) or **Maqra'/Rubu'** (1/4 Hizb), ensuring the semantic meaning of the verse is not broken.
- **Setoran Scope:** Users define their setoran not just by Surah, but structurally (e.g., "I want to submit Maqra' 12" or "I want to submit Pages 50-52").

## II. Smart Worship Tracker (The "Strava" of Ibadah)
- **Halaman (Page) Metrics:** Tracks progress based on the 604 Pages of the Madinah Mushaf. Visualizes how many pages are read daily.
- **Khatam Planners:**
  - *Juz Mode (30 Days):* Perfect for Ramadan. Tracks 1 Juz per day.
  - *Manzil Mode (7 Days):* For advanced Hafidz. Tracks 1 Manzil per day to complete the Quran in a week.
- **Exportable "Action Cards":** Generates graphics like "Just Finished Hizb 5! 🚀" for social media.

## III. Social Map & Mosque Explorer
- **Live Activities & Check-in Ibadah:** Map showing Kajian, Buka Puasa, and Volunteer needs. Check-in system for streaks.

## IV. Sparing Tilawah (Matchmaking)
*Goal: Gamified Memorization Testing*
- **Filter by Structural Level:** Users can filter their matchmaking pool. Example: "Find me an opponent to test Juz 30" or "Let's spar on Hizb 10".
- **Sambung Ayat (Ayat-by-Ayat):** Because the database relies on the 6,236 Ayahs, the AI instantly pulls the *next* Ayah globally and prompts the user to continue exactly where the opponent left off.

## V. Talent Hub (Imam & Muazin)
- **Verified Portfolios:** Audio snippets verified by Ustadz.
- **DKM Dashboard:** Search for Imams based on location, voice type, and verification level.
