# AI CURRICULUM & METHODOLOGY (MADINAH/MAKKAH STANDARD)
*Inspired by Qaraa Ecosystem & Tarteel's Memorization Logic*

## 1. The 7-Stage Curriculum (Sanad Framework)
To train the AI and guide the user, the app implements a structured 7-stage progression model adopted from the formal Islamic Universities in Makkah and Madinah.

**Level 1: Makharijul Huruf (Articulation Points)**
- *AI Task:* Detect subtle differences (e.g., distinguishing between `Sin` س and `Syin` ش).

**Level 2: Ahkam At-Tajwid (Reading Rules)**
- *AI Task:* Measure audio duration (milliseconds) to ensure Mad is exactly 2, 4, or 6 harakat. Detect nasalization (Ghunnah).

**Level 3: Hifz & Tilawah (Memorization - *Tarteel Style*)**
- *AI Task:* Real-time Mistake Detection (flagging skipped words, wrong harakat). Includes automated **Memorization Planning**.

**Level 4: Mufradat (Quranic Vocabulary / Harfiah)**
**Level 5: Nahwu & Sharf (Grammar & Morphology)**
**Level 6 & 7: Tafsir & Tadabbur (Commentary & Reflection)**

## 2. The Structural Intelligence Engine (The Mushaf Architecture)
The AI is not blind; it understands the exact 1400-year-old architectural divisions of the Quran. This dictates how the AI assigns tasks and evaluates logic:
1. **Juz (30 Parts):** The AI uses this for generating 30-Day macro-plans (e.g., Ramadan Targets) and matching users in the Sparing feature.
2. **Manzil (7 Parts):** The AI generates ultra-hardcore 7-Day targets for verified Hafidz.
3. **Hizb (60 Parts) & Maqra' (240 Parts):** The AI defines a single "Setoran Session" optimally around 1 Maqra' (a quarter of a Hizb). This prevents cognitive overload for the Ustadz reviewing the audio.
4. **Ruku' (556 Thematic Stops):** The AI **warns the user** if they press "Stop Recording" in the middle of a Ruku', as stopping mid-theme can distort the Tafsir (meaning). The AI encourages users to stop only at the 'Ain mark.
5. **Ayat (6,236 Units):** The atomic layer. The AI detects mistakes strictly per-Ayat and uses it for the Omegle-style "Lanjut Ayat" sparring.
6. **Halaman (604 Pages - Madinah Standard):** The primary UI metric for the "Strava-like" progress tracker (e.g., "You read 5 pages today").
