      # BUSINESS CASE & VALUE PROPOSITION (GLOBAL SCALE)

## 1. The Global TAM (Total Addressable Market)
**Target Market:** The Global Muslim Population (Projected to reach **2.2 Billion by 2030** and eventually **3 Billion**).
While Indonesia serves as the ultimate launchpad (largest Muslim population), this product is engineered from Day-1 for **Global Domination**. The pain points of learning the Quran and building community are universal across the Middle East, Europe, North America, and South Asia.

## 2. Problem Statement
1. **Inefficiency in Verification (Sanad):** Finding a verified Ustadz for *Setoran* is globally restricted. Muslims in minority areas (e.g., Europe, US) have extreme difficulty finding Sanad-verified teachers.
2. **Isolation of Worship:** Islamic worship is largely isolated. Without positive social pressure (peer motivation), retention drops.
3. **Lack of Standardization:** Few apps implement strict, internationally recognized Tajwid standards (Kurikulum Makkah/Madinah) accessible in multiple languages.
4. **Mosque Disconnection:** Mosques worldwide lack digital infrastructure to engage Generation Z.

## 3. Value Proposition
* **For Users (Global):** "Learn with world-class AI, verify with a human Ustadz anywhere in the world, and stay motivated through a global community." 
* **For Ustadz:** "A Super-Dashboard that cuts review time by 70%, allowing you to mentor students globally across different timezones."
* **For Mosques (Global):** "A live radar to boost jama'ah attendance and a verified talent pool to find Imams globally."
